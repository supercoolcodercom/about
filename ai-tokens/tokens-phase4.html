Phase 4: Use the Clues Around It

Today's idea: A language model uses the words around a prediction to help decide what comes next.

This phase introduces context and attention using small visual experiments.

Context
The words and information around the part we are trying to predict.
Clue
A word that may help the prediction.
Attention
Giving more focus to some clues and less focus to others.

Important: This laboratory uses a simplified teaching model. It helps you see the idea of attention, but it is not showing the actual calculations used inside a real Transformer.

Activity 1: Context Matters

Context means the information around what the model is trying to predict.

Look at the same ending in two different situations.

Sentence A

The robot went to the garage and opened the ___.

Sentence B

The robot went to the kitchen and opened the ___.
THINK
The last few words before the blank were not the same. Did the useful clues change?

Activity 2: Change the Context

Change one part of the sentence. Then see how the next-word prediction changes.

The robot walked into the ___ and looked around.
YOUR NEXT STEP
Choose room. Show the prediction. Then choose kitchen and show it again.

Activity 3: Attention Is Focusing

The model does not need every word to be equally useful. In this simple example, some words give stronger clues than others.

Simple meaning:
Attention means giving more focus to some words and less focus to others.
THINK
Which words received the strongest focus? Why might those words be useful for predicting what happens next?

Activity 4: Use Several Clues

A prediction can use more than one useful clue at the same time.

YOUR NEXT STEP
Look at all the highlighted words. Notice that the model can use several clues together, not just one word.

Challenge: What Should the Model Focus On?

Read the sentence. Imagine that the model wants to predict what happens next.

The robot carried the heavy box into the dark room.

Choose the 2 or 3 words you think would be useful clues for predicting what happens next.

Remember: A useful clue is not a guarantee. Attention can help a model focus, but it does not mean the model perfectly understands the world.
