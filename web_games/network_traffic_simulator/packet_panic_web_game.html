How to Play Packet Panic

Master packet routing and survive network stress!

• Packet Panic simulates a real-world computer network where data packets travel from source Senders to matching destination Receivers.

• As the network architect, your job is to build efficient routes using Routers and Cable Lines before network queues overflow.

• Beware of network stress—cable severances and router overloads will delay packets and test your network redundancy under pressure!

1. Placing Routers

Select Place Router from the toolbox and click anywhere on the grid canvas to position intermediate routing nodes.

2. Drawing Cables

Select Draw Cable, then click and drag between nodes to establish physical data connections.

3. Routing Packets

Color-coded packets automatically spawn at Senders. Routers automatically forward packets toward matching Receivers along connected paths.

4. Dynamic Timer & Challenge Progression

• First-time players get a relaxed 15 minutes limit to get comfortable with routing.
• Complete the 15-minute introductory mode to unlock the 1 Minute Challenge!
• If you fail a challenge, gain +1 minute extra on your next try.
• Succeeding reduces time towards the ultimate 1 Minute (60s) speed-run floor.
